Q) What are tags and attributes in HTML?
A) Tags are the primary component of the HTML that defines 
   how the content will be structured/ formatted, whereas
   Attributes are used along with the HTML tags to define
   the characteristics of the element. For example, 
   <p align=” center”>Interview questions</p>, in this the
   ‘align’ is the attribute using which we will align the 
   paragraph to show in the center of the view.
